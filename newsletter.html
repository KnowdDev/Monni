Maker of the Month

Frolic Ceramics

Vicki Fanning, Matakana

[ IMAGE — Frolic jugs & mugs on the Tea & Tonic back bench, styled with blooms ]

We're grateful to walk alongside Vicki Fanning of Frolic Ceramics, a treasured local ceramicist and glass artist who has been part of our story since the very beginning. Her pieces are functional while still holding an organic, unrepeatable form — no two are ever quite the same.

Her famous water jugs, often first spotted at The Brewery, are one of our favourites. You'll find them filled with blooms on our back bench, alongside soap dishes and mugs crafted for comfort and quiet moments of solace. Every piece is a small ritual in itself.

We sat down with Vicki to talk childhood farms, the seduction of glass, and building a creative life around a growing son.

Tea of the Week

Ruby Chai

By Amber & Gold
