If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more relaxed fit, or sizing down for a closer fit. Fit may vary slightly by style — refer to individual product pages for specific fit notes.

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal. Do not pull too tightly.

Waist

Measure around your natural waist at the narrowest point, just above your belly button. Keep the tape relaxed.

Hips

Stand with feet together and measure around the widest part of your hips, approximately 20cm below your natural waist.

For the most accurate results, use a soft measuring tape and wear minimal clothing. Ask a friend to help for a more precise measurement. All measurements refer to body measurements, not garment measurements.
