Tea of the Month

Gloria

A nourishing blend created to support healthy hair from within, combining mineral-rich nettle and oatstraw with spearmint and peppermint. Bright and restorative, it's designed as a daily ritual, nourishing the body from the inside out to support strength and natural radiance.

Key ingredients: Nettle, oatstraw, spearmint — all certified organic.

Tea ritual: Preheat your pot with boiling water to bloom the flavour. Add a heaped teaspoon per cup, simmer for five minutes, then leave to steep for five more. Once brewed, return the spent herbs to the earth — compost, a worm farm, or the garden — and let the cooled leftover tea nourish your plants.

Available in 25g, 50g and 100g. Made in Aotearoa, New Zealand.
