# Monni Brand Foundation

## Brand Essence

**Core Philosophy**
The website should feel like an extension of the in-store experience. It should carry the same calm, thoughtful energy that people feel the moment they walk through the doors. A sense of softness, beauty, warmth, and care. The experience should feel elevated yet grounding, minimal yet emotionally rich. It should invite people to slow down, breathe deeper, and move through the space with ease.

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Soft neutrals, natural textures, pale stone tones
- **Secondary**: Washed timber hues, gentle pink undertones (from macrocarpa shelving)
- **Overall Feeling**: Light, feminine, and tranquil without becoming overly delicate or "pretty"
- **Positioning**: Natural luxury and quiet restraint
- **Balance**: Holistic and earthy, but never overly bohemian or stereotypically wellness-based
- **Edge**: Modernity, simplicity, and understated sophistication that gives the brand depth and distinction

### Design Influences
- **Japanese Scandinavian aesthetic**: Strong influence throughout
- **Minimalist approach**: Clean, spacious with room to breathe
- **Refined simplicity**: Every image, word, and interaction has intention behind it
- **Timeless quality**: Not trend-driven or overworked

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Personality
- **Calm**: Tranquil and soothing
- **Thoughtful**: Intentional and considered
- **Elevated**: Sophisticated without being unattainable
- **Grounding**: Rooted in care and authenticity
- **Warm**: Human and personal

### Communication Style
- **Emotional layer**: Not overly sentimental, but human and personal
- **Grounded in care**: Ritual, beauty, and wellbeing
- **Small details matter**: Every interaction is carefully thought through
- **Clear and simple**: Information should be easy to find and elegantly presented

## Website Experience

### Homepage Design
- **Visual hierarchy**: Strong imagery should lead the experience
- **Products first**: Allow products, textures, atmosphere, and feeling to speak first
- **Clean layout**: Spacious with room to breathe
- **Design elements**: Large imagery, split screen moments, soft typography, subtle movement
- **Immersive quality**: Creates a feeling of immersion without overwhelming the user

### Navigation & UX
- **Curated experience**: Calm, intentional, and uncluttered like the physical store
- **Intuitive shopping**: Natural movement between categories (gifting, beauty, holistic wellbeing, homewares, clothing, jewellery)
- **Friction-free**: Easy navigation without complexity
- **Elegant footer**: Shipping, returns, contact details, and social links elegantly laid out

### Emotional Connection
- **Sanctuary online**: A place that reflects both beauty and depth
- **Blended experience**: Holistic living, thoughtful design, refined simplicity, emotional connection
- **Feminine without fragile**: Strong yet soft
- **Natural without "hippie"**: Grounded authenticity
- **Luxurious without unattainable**: Accessible sophistication
- **Modern with warmth**: Contemporary but soulful

## Product Categories

### Core Categories
- Gifting
- Beauty
- Holistic wellbeing
- Homewares
- Clothing
- Jewellery

### Presentation
- Each category should feel naturally connected
- Visual consistency across all product types
- Strong imagery that represents the fragrance/product experience (critical for scent-based products)
- Photography and branding should be exceptional and emotionally resonant

## Technical Requirements

### Performance Priorities
- **Hyper-optimised**: Fast loading and smooth interactions
- **Mobile-first**: Seamless experience across all devices
- **Accessibility**: Clear typography, good contrast, intuitive navigation
- **SEO-friendly**: Proper structure, meta tags, fast performance

### User Experience Principles
- **Simple and beautiful**: Avoid clunky, busy, or overwhelming designs
- **Easy to navigate**: Clear paths to purchase
- **Trust signals**: Clear shipping, returns, and contact information
- **Newsletter integration**: Beautifully designed, integrated rather than intrusive

## Brand References & Inspiration

### Aesthetic References
- **Assembly Label (NZ)**: https://nz.assemblylabel.com/
- **Able Fragrance**: Exceptional photography and branding, strong image usage
- **Frama CPH**: Design edge consideration (though user prefers less design-heavy approach)

### What to Avoid
- Drop-down menus that feel clunky or outdated
- Overly busy or complex navigation
- Trend-driven design that won't age well
- Cheap or low-quality aesthetic
- Overwhelming user experience

## Design Philosophy

**Core Principle**
The website should feel like a sanctuary online. A place that blends holistic living, thoughtful design, refined simplicity, and emotional connection. Every element should be intentional, beautiful, and deeply considered.

**Success Metrics**
- Users feel calm and relaxed while browsing
- Navigation is intuitive and frictionless
- Visual consistency reinforces brand identity
- Emotional connection drives engagement and conversion
- Performance is fast and seamless
- The experience feels like an extension of the physical store

## Implementation Notes

### Shopify Theme Development
- **Foundation**: Shopify Skeleton Theme
- **Approach**: Minimal, performant, custom-built on top of skeleton
- **Customization**: Tailored to brand aesthetic while maintaining Shopify best practices
- **Optimization**: Focus on Core Web Vitals, image optimization, minimal JavaScript

### Content Strategy
- **Imagery-led**: Strong photography and visual storytelling
- **Copy**: Minimal, intentional, emotionally resonant
- **Product presentation**: Focus on texture, atmosphere, and feeling
- **Storytelling**: Weave emotional narrative throughout the experience
