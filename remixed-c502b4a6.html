Tea & Tonic

Welcome

We welcome you to a community that believes in
things made with care: sustainable, woman-led,
and proudly New Zealand made.

Tea & Tonic began with a simple belief: that wellbeing, beauty,
and the way we live are all a part of the same ritual. We favour
natural materials, slower processes, and the women-led,
small-batch makers who share the belief that everything is
connected, and that the health of people and planet go
hand in hand.

Thank you for being here.

Aroha nui, Monique

A space for holistic health, beauty & home.
